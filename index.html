O namaAbout us

Smash Daddy je otvoren 6. februara 2026. godine u Retail Parku Vrbas. Od tada pravimo smash burgere od 100% junećeg mesa iz lokalne mesare.Smash Daddy opened on 6 February 2026 in Retail Park Vrbas. Since then we have been making smash burgers from 100% beef out of a local butcher shop.

Na meniju imamo 9 različitih smash burgera, a ako više voliš piletinu, tu su i pileći sendviči. Uz to možeš da izabereš pomfrit, sos ili hladno piće.The menu has 9 different smash burgers, and if you prefer chicken, there are chicken sandwiches too. With that you can pick fries, a sauce or a cold drink.
